South Clan Football Academy's 18 assignments return `NOTHING` on every field.

`IKF-S6-006` "IKF Scout on Wheel" (49 cities) and `IKF-S6-008` "IKF Scout on
Wheel Naari Shakti" (20) are **separate programmes sharing 18 cities**, created
42 minutes apart. One row per project-city is correct. Rajahmundry, Singrauli,
Gwalior, Bikaner, Ganganagar all appear twice for that reason.

### Courier trial dates — 53 shipments

    date visible to users              41
    assignment deleted, unrecoverable   9
    orphaned (city not in the project)  3

The 2 that looked broken (`CR-2026-0027` Siwan, `CR-2026-0026` Ahmednagar) hold

returns 2026-04-26 and 2026-10-04. Verified.

The 9 unrecoverable ones each have a date in ANOTHER project. A trial date
belongs to a (project, city) pair — Singrauli is 3 Jul under S6-006 and 10 Jul
under S6-008 — so copying one would be inventing.
