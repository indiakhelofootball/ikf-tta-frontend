Amber is most legible here — #FBBF24 on near-black clears contrast easily, where on cream it never could. Caveat: dark signals "developer tool," which may be wrong for funders.

Which I'd pick, and why

01 for staff, 02 for funders. Direction 01 keeps everything you already own and fixes the actual defect — it's a background colour and a button colour, deployable this week. Direction 02 is where I'd take the funder-facing portal, because a corporate CSR head deciding on next year's grant reads cool neutrals as competence and warm saturation as informality.

Every direction above shares one change, and it's the whole diagnosis: three clear value tiers, and a primary button that is the darkest or most saturated thing in the form. On the live page the button is the palest element and measures 1.21:1 against its own panel, which is why it reads as disabled.

What I dropped: a glassmorphism direction — it failed my own contrast check on every text layer and couldn't be fixed without abandoning the effect that defined it.
